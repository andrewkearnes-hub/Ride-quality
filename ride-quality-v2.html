Method & limitations — read before quoting these figures

Gravity is locked during a stationary calibration and all motion projected onto true vertical, so orientation doesn't matter. Vibration is band-limited with a zero-phase Butterworth filter, then reported as A95 and peak-to-peak in milli-g. Jerk comes from a 0.07 s smoothed profile. Speed and distance come from integrating vertical acceleration with an end-point zero-velocity constraint.

What this cannot do
  • Calibrated absolute values. Phone MEMS sensors have no traceable calibration — treat readings as indicative, not certifiable.
  • Levelling accuracy. Millimetre floor-level error needs a position reference; integration drift is far too large.
  • Frequencies above ~30 Hz. iOS caps sampling near 60 Hz, so higher-frequency roller and bearing noise is invisible.
  • True dBA. Phone microphones are uncalibrated and compressed; sound figures are relative only.
  • Automatic door timing. Use the event marker button to time door and flight events manually.
